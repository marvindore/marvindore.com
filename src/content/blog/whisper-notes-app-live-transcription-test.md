In the video above, I tested the Whisper Notes app live at a conference by recording real talks and checking what it produced. I said you'd get the full audio and transcript for every talk I showed, and this page is where they live.

Jump to [the audio and transcripts](#talks) below. Each talk has an audio player, the full transcript, and downloads for the audio, a plain-text transcript and an `.srt` captions file.

## My verdict on the app

It works well, and I'd recommend it. The things I like most:

- **It's 100 percent local.** The transcription happens on your own machine.
- **One flat fee, and you own it forever.** It costs $7.99 as of November 14, 2026, with no subscription.
- **Mistakes are easy to fix.** A few words were transcribed incorrectly, but the app keeps the audio next to the transcript, so you can edit the script whenever you want.

One limitation: it's only available on iOS right now, so if you're on Android or Windows, it's not an option yet.

## How to use the players

- **Press play** and the line being spoken is highlighted in the transcript. Leave "Follow along" on and the transcript scrolls with it.
- **Click any sentence**, or the timestamp above a paragraph, to jump the audio to that moment.
- **Use the download links** if you want to keep the audio or search the text in your own editor.

## A note on accuracy

These transcripts come from the test itself, so a few words are wrong. Names, product terms and crosstalk are the usual trouble spots. For example, the Steve Stoute transcript hears "AfroTech" as "Afro technical" and "Afro time zone". Check anything important against the audio, which is exactly what the click-to-seek player is for.
